Reading the tree

Ancestors rise above the person in the centre, descendants fall below. A gold ring marks whoever is in focus. Colour follows the family name, so you can see where the Chereks marry into the Fandres and where the Meirose line comes in from Nebraska.

Everything here comes from the GEDCOM your grandfather built. Where two records disagreed about a date or a place, both are shown — look for “according to another record”.
